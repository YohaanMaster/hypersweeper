How to play

Hypersweeper is minesweeper played on a 4-dimensional board. There are 256 cells residing on a 4x4x4x4 hypercube, with each cell having up to 80 neighbours. In the layer view, you can see all 16 "slices" that make up the hypercube, while in the rotate view you can visualise it as a projection in 3D space.

Like regular minesweeper, left click on a cell to reveal how many of its neighbours are mines, and right click to flag it as a suspected mine. Left clicking a revealed cell which has all the mines around it flagged with reveal its remaining neighbours.
